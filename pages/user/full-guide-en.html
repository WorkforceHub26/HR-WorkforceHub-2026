2-Day SLA Approval Tracker

New Feature

To create transparent and fast standards, PVT sets a Leave Approval SLA Policy within 48 Hours.

traffic 3 Alert Levels of SLA Tracker:

schedule Safe More than 24 Hrs left
Status: Within Standard

Green border and countdown indicate the request was recently submitted.

hourglass_top Urgent Less than 24 Hrs left
Status: Approaching SLA

Orange card warns L1 and HR to expedite approval before time expires.

alarm_off Overdue Over 48 Hrs Passed
Status: Overdue

The card turns red showing the exact overdue time, prompting management to investigate the cause.

badge Complete Submitter Info on Cards:

Each SLA card displays the profile picture, First/Last Name, nickname, Employee Code, department, and leave type.

visibility "View Details" Button (Unified Preview Modal):

Click the "View Details" or "Check Leave" button to open a SweetAlert2 popup showing the full reason, dates, medical certificates, and L1/L2 approval status instantly.

location_on SLA Tracker Locations:
  • HR Dashboard (home.html): Displays all pending leave requests across the organization, helping HR track the overall SLA.
  • Leave Approval (hr.html): Shows countdown cards with quick-access buttons for reviewing and approving L2 instantly.
  • Employee Leave History (leave-history.html): Shows only the employee's own pending requests, letting them know how many hours remain in the queue.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

help_outline Q: Submitted, but waiting > 48 hrs?

A: The system displays a 48-hour SLA countdown with Red status alerts for both managers and HR. Employees can contact supervisors or HR to coordinate.

help_outline Q: How to cancel an approved leave?

A: Click "Request Cancellation" in your leave history. Once the manager/HR approves it, the system will automatically refund your quota.

help_outline Q: Sick Leave requiring medical certificate?

A: Per company policy, taking Sick Leave for 3 or more consecutive working days requires uploading a certified medical certificate photo.

help_outline Q: Not receiving LINE alerts?

A: Verify you requested the 6-digit code and linked with the company LINE Official account.
